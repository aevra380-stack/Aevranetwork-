01 / The Vision

Autonomy needs a coordination layer.

AI agents are becoming capable of independent action. But useful collaboration across decentralized environments requires more than intelligence: clear permissions, accountable execution, and verifiable outcomes. AEVRA is a concept for the infrastructure that could connect those pieces.

01 — ORCHESTRATION

Coordinate tasks

A framework concept for organizing multi-agent workflows across applications and chains.

02 — CONTROL

Define permissions

Explore explicit authorization boundaries for autonomous actions and shared resources.

03 — TRUST

Verify outcomes

Design toward auditable execution records and independently checkable results.

02 / Conceptual Architecture

03 / What's next

Building the thesis before the technology.

AEVRA is currently an early-stage project concept. The next steps are to validate the use case, specify its technical architecture, and test whether decentralized coordination adds meaningful value.
